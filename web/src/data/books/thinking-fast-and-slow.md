# Thinking, Fast and Slow

## Description

The major New York Times bestseller that changed the way we think about thinking. In his international bestseller, Thinking, Fast and Slow, Daniel Kahneman, the renowned psychologist and winner of the Nobel Prize in Economics, takes us on a groundbreaking tour of the mind and explains the two systems that drive the way we think.

## Key Takeaways

- System 1 is fast, intuitive, and emotional; System 2 is slower, more deliberative, and more logical
- We are susceptible to cognitive biases that affect our decision-making
- Understanding these systems can help us make better choices
