# Sapiens

A Brief History of Humankind

## Description

From a renowned historian comes a groundbreaking narrative of humanity's creation and evolution—a #1 international bestseller—that explores the ways in which biology and history have defined us and enhanced our understanding of what it means to be "human."

## Key Takeaways

- The Cognitive Revolution gave Homo sapiens the edge over other species
- The Agricultural Revolution was both a blessing and a curse
- The Scientific Revolution transformed our understanding of the world
- Shared myths and beliefs enable large-scale human cooperation
